GH
How do you scale past 10k concurrent users without going broke?
asked by grace_h2 days ago▲ 148 votes

We're hitting connection limits nightly. Vertical scaling doubled our bill and bought us three weeks. What actually works at this size?

AL
✓ Accepted Answer ada_l▲ 231 votes

Three moves, in order of cost-effectiveness: cache the top 20 queries (we measured 71% of load), move session state out of the app tier, then add read replicas. Do them in that order — most teams buy replicas first and wonder why the bill grew faster than the headroom. Full cost math in the thread.

LB
linus_b▲ 96 votes

Adding to ada's answer: profile before caching. Our "top queries" intuition was wrong in four of five cases.
