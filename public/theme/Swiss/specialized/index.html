Security & Regulation

The questions our compliance team loves to answer.

Is my money insured?

Yes — deposits are insured up to $250,000 per depositor under the national scheme, and client investments are held in segregated custody.

Who regulates Nexus Trust?

We hold a full banking charter and are supervised by the national banking authority, with public audit reports filed twice a year.

How is my data protected?

End-to-end encryption in transit and at rest, biometric authentication, and no sale of customer data — ever, to anyone.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Freeze everything from any browser in under a minute, then restore access with in-app identity re-verification.
