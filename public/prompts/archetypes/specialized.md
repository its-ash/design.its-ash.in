# Archetype: Specialized Platform

**Covers**: banking, healthcare, LMS, gaming, government, real estate.

Build the page as **Nexus Trust**, a regulated digital bank (the trust-heaviest
member of the category). Swap the vertical while keeping the structure.

Anatomy:

1. **Split hero** (`#hero`, `arch-split--wide-left`) — left: chartered/est. badge, trust headline, fiduciary standfirst, meta row of trust markers (deposit insurance, ISO cert, public audits). Right: **Online Banking card** — Sign In (opens login popup), Open an Account (signup popup), Forgot credentials (toast), divider, security fine print. The login card is the signature: no other archetype has one.
2. **Today's Rates** (`#features`, `arch-split--flip`) — rates table (product / APR / term / minimum / availability tag) beside a Featured Rate card with an oversized APY figure set in `.price` and an open-deposit CTA.
3. **What We Do** — three zigzag rows (`arch-split` alternating `--flip`): kicker, h3, paragraph, outline CTA, paired with an icon card carrying a benefits meta row. Rows, not a card grid.
4. **Opening an Account** — the only timeline on any archetype: Minute 0 → Minute 5 → Hour 1 → Day 1.
5. **Inside the App** — the mandatory slider, five app screens.
6. **Security & Regulation** — the only accordion on any archetype, answered in compliance voice.
7. **Book an Appointment** (`#contact`) — the mandatory contact form; subject select becomes Department.
8. **Fine print** — a closing `arch-fineprint` legal paragraph. Mandatory for this archetype; forbidden on the others.

Signature elements: login card, big APY, regulator language, timeline, legal footer.
Tone: fiduciary, precise, reassuring.
